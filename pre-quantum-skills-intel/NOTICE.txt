The files in this directory are source research that informed the public
funder data used by the quantum grant matching system.

Some files reference specific organisations as research context. These
references are for internal provenance only. The extracted funder data
(amounts, deadlines, eligibility criteria) is public information sourced
from funder websites and published guidelines.

The system's runtime code does NOT read these files. They are retained
as a record of where the enrichment data originated.
